# Prompt 07 — Marketing Site

## Goal

Explain the product and convert interested parents. Corresponds to Roadmap
Phases 1–6 (marketing surface of the MVP).

## Read first

- `docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md`
- `docs/MVP_SCOPE.md` (marketing experience)
- `docs/PRIVACY_AND_SAFETY.md`

## Task

1. Build the homepage with the product explanation and core promise.
2. Add a sample interactive scenario playable without an account.
3. Add an email waitlist or account-creation entry point.
4. Add a pricing placeholder.
5. Add a clear privacy and safety explanation for parents.

## Constraints

- Warm, calm, trustworthy tone aimed at parents.
- No advertising, no third-party tracking pixels, no dark patterns.
- The sample scenario must not require or collect child personal data.
- Do not imitate competitors' branding or layouts.

## Definition of done

- Marketing pages are responsive and accessible.
- Sample scenario works with no account.
- Waitlist/account entry point works and validates input.
- Lint, typecheck, tests, and build pass.
