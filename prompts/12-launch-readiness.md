# Prompt 12 — Launch Readiness

## Goal

Confirm the MVP is ready for a paid family beta. Corresponds to Roadmap
Phases 7–8.

## Read first

- `docs/MVP_SCOPE.md` (completion criteria)
- `docs/ROADMAP.md` (Phases 7–8)
- `docs/PRIVACY_AND_SAFETY.md` (legal note)
- `docs/ANALYTICS.md`

## Task

1. Verify every MVP completion criterion in `MVP_SCOPE.md` end to end.
2. Validate that analytics events fire correctly and answer the initial
   questions in `ANALYTICS.md`.
3. Confirm error monitoring, performance, and the beta support process.
4. Prepare the founding-family beta onboarding.
5. Compile the open legal-review items (COPPA, state and international privacy,
   app-store family policies, subscription and auto-renewal rules) for qualified
   human review before a public child-directed launch.

## Constraints

- Do not claim professional, educational, or clinical validation that has not
  occurred.
- Curriculum content remains draft until reviewed by qualified humans.
- Do not launch to the public without the required legal review.

## Definition of done

- All MVP completion criteria demonstrably met.
- Analytics validated; monitoring in place.
- Legal-review checklist compiled and handed off.
- Lint, typecheck, tests, and build pass.
