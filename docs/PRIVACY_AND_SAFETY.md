# Privacy and Safety

## Product posture

The product should collect the minimum data necessary to deliver the service.

## Account model

- The parent or guardian owns the account.
- Child profiles exist under the parent account.
- Children do not provide email addresses.
- Children do not have independent credentials.
- Child display names may be nicknames.
- Do not require full legal names.
- Do not require birth dates.
- Store an age band rather than an exact birthday when possible.

## Prohibited without explicit legal review

- Advertising to children
- Behavioral advertising
- Third-party tracking pixels
- Sale of personal information
- Public child profiles
- Child-to-child communication
- Precise location
- Contact collection
- Biometric information
- Voiceprint storage
- Facial analysis
- Unmoderated user-generated content

## Analytics

Collect first-party product events such as:

- Parent account created
- Child profile created
- Lesson started
- Lesson completed
- Mission viewed
- Mission marked complete
- Parent dashboard viewed

Do not store unnecessary free-form child responses.

## Deletion

Parents must be able to:

- Delete a child profile
- Delete lesson progress
- Delete their account
- Request data export

## Content safety

Lessons must not teach children to remain polite during unsafe interactions.

Safety, consent, and trusted-adult guidance take priority.

## Legal note

Repository documentation and code do not constitute legal advice.

Before a public child-directed launch, obtain qualified review concerning:

- COPPA
- State privacy laws
- International privacy requirements where applicable
- App-store child and family policies
- Subscription and auto-renewal rules
