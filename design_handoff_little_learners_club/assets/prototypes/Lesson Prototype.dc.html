Little Learner's Club — Phase 7 · interactive prototype

Hello Garden · Saying Hello

A tappable child flow built entirely on the Storybook Geometry system and Little Motions. Start at launch, cross the clubhouse and map, walk the learning path, and play the full lesson: scene → choice → gentle consequence → principle → practice → offline mission → celebration. Every choice shows a believable, non-judgemental outcome. Use the controls to reset, jump screens, flip reduced motion, or turn on the nonverbal alternative.
