Glim

the clubhouse lantern · companion, not judge
An old brass lantern that has hung by the clubhouse door long enough to get curious. Glim can't talk — it notices, wonders, glows, and lights the way. It asks questions with a tilt and a wonder-bubble; it never declares anyone good or bad, and it never dims because of a child's choice.
Guide rules: asks, never grades · models curiosity first ("I wonder…") · reacts gently and briefly · carries transitions (its light leads to the next place) · works with zero spoken dialogue — wonder-bubbles hold an icon plus narrated text.
Form rules: 1.6 heads tall · the only sanctioned glow in the system · dot eyes in the glass, one mouth stroke · no limbs — expression lives in tilt, hop, and glow intensity.
Unique states: glow_notice · wonder_tilt · path_light · dim_rest (never triggered by a choice) · plus the shared library where body allows.
Never: a bird, an owl, a scorekeeper, a nag. Glim never blocks the screen, never repeats a prompt more than once, and never celebrates louder than the child's own moment.
