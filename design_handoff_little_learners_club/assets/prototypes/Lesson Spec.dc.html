Little Learner's Club — Phase 7 · implementation specification

Saying Hello — screen by screen

The engineering companion to Lesson Prototype. One card per screen: purpose, the components it composes (existing Kindlyo primitives named where they apply, new where they must be built), layout & responsive rules, interactions, states, motion tokens, reduced-motion, accessibility, and content rules. Everything references the Storybook Geometry tokens and Little Motions — no screen introduces a one-off value.

Breakpoints
phone < 600px — single column, bottom nav, one action in view.
tablet ≥ 600px — lesson uses a two-pane split (scene ‖ interaction); chrome gets wider gutters (space-12).
No layout below 360px; nothing horizontally scrolls at 375 / 768 / 1024.
Global rules
Touch targets ≥ 44×44 with ≥ space-2 between. One h1 per screen, no skipped levels. Focus moves to the step region on step change (not first render). Nothing is conveyed by color alone. No correct/wrong/rude anywhere.
