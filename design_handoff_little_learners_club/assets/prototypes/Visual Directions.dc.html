Vector characters are working sketches; final art is an illustrator's job. Comparison and recommendation at the bottom.

Recommendation
A hybrid: Graphic Clubhouse bones, Modern Storybook skin

Take from 1c: the modular vector construction — shape-grid characters, geometric scenery kits, segmented progress. It is the cheapest to animate across 12 module worlds, the most readable at phone size, and the only direction that ages up cleanly to tween and adult products.

Take from 1a: the warmth — cream/terracotta/sage/honey palette (an evolution of the existing Kindlyo tokens), the serif display voice, layered paper depth behind key moments, and the editorial parent surface that earns adult trust.

Take from 1b: one thing only — the soft squish as the touch-response feel in Little Motions. Tactile feedback without committing to dimensional art everywhere.

Hybrid at a glance

Palette + serif from 1a
