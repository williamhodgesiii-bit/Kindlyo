Little Learner's Club — module worlds & storyboards

The Neighborhood — twelve worlds, one kit

Every module world is a place in one living neighborhood, introduced by one repeatable six-beat formula and built from one shared scenery kit. Target held: by scene instance, ≥80% of drawn elements are kit parts; each world adds at most four unique assets. Cultural difference is represented as variation between homes, tables, and gardens — never as one standard with exceptions.
